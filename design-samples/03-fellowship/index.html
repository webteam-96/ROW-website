What is Roster On Wheels?

Roster On Wheels is club management software built only for Rotary. It is made by Kaizen Infotech Solutions, which has run Rotary club and district administration in India for over twelve years. ROW connects to the Rotary International database, so your roster and officers come straight from My Rotary instead of being typed in twice.

Plan next year

Plan next year from January, not July

Most Rotary software only works for the year you are already in. With ROW, a district governor elect can sign up in January and start building the coming Rotary year straight away: appoint the district team, set goals, publish the calendar and open PETS and SETS registration. Every incoming club president and secretary gets a next-year workspace automatically. Your current year keeps running on your current system until the 1 July changeover.

Your next-year launchpad

  • District team appointed
  • District goals set
  • District calendar published
  • PETS and SETS scheduled, with registration open
  • Clubs invited
  • Clubs authorized with Rotary International
  • This Rotary year: stays on your current system

Switch to ROW

Moving from ClubRunner or DACdb?

Your member data lives at Rotary International, so your roster and officers come across through My Rotary. We import the rest: attendance history, balances, contacts, events and documents. Your secretary signs a reconciliation report before anything goes live, and your old system keeps running until then. Standard migration is free and takes ten working days.

FAQ

Questions Rotarians ask us

Is ROW connected to My Rotary?

Yes. ROW syncs members, officers and club details with Rotary International once your club designates ROW in My Rotary.

Can we try it first?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 60-day trial with full access, and nothing you set up is lost when you subscribe.

Do members need to log in on a website?

No. Members use the ROW app on iPhone or Android, and officers use the web console.
