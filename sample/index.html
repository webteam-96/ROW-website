WHAT IS ROSTER ON WHEELS?

Roster On Wheels is a Rotary International Official Licensee mobile application that connects 1.2 million fellow Rotarians globally through a single platform. It is a club management tool that helps Presidents and Secretaries run their club in a far more organised way, using the E-Governance, Public Image and Communication tools built into it.

Why the change?
Carry your club’s digitised roster on your phone and pull up any member’s details in a few clicks. Stay on top of every upcoming event in your club and your district, without chasing paper.

Run the club properly with less effort: RSVPs for club events, dues collection, the monthly reports you file with the district, and your club’s OCV. Trade physical paperwork and manual chasing for a digital platform, and save what both cost.
